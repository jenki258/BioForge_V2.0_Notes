## Overview

This instrument performs one field or laboratory task and complements the other diagnostic tools.
It is the inexpensive, low-durability entry point to blood collection.
## Obtaining

- **crafting shaped** (`wooden_needle.json`): `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/flint`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/stick`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/string` → 1 × Wooden Needle.

## Function

## Progression connections

## Stored data and safety

- It carries no readable biological payload by default, although contaminated ingredients can still propagate a strain.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:wooden_needle`.
