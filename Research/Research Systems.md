## Research item roles

| Item | Role |
|---|---|
| Virus Sample / Live Culture Vial / Petri Dish | Carries an infection strain into analysis or synthesis. |
| Medical Report | Exact-strain clinical evidence and complete blood research. |
| Gene Imprint | Captures one mutation, transmission, or symptom target. |
| CRISPR Cartridge | Holds four bases and one of 15 program positions. |
| Cas Module | Defines PAM, efficiency, and compatibility. |
| CRISPR Notes | Stores a reusable complete sequence template. |
| Vaccine carrier | Determines the family of the requested output. |

## Separation of knowledge

- The Clipboard knows the patient and observed findings.
- A Medical Report proves clinical research for an exact strain.
- The Microscope identifies hidden biological targets.
- CRISPR Notes remember a sequence but do not certify patient evidence.
- The Strain Catalogue provides a public name but not the internal genes.
- The Vaccine Maker estimates quality but does not explain every hidden correction.

This separation connects the machines: no single item replaces the whole laboratory.
