# Information Items

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Item #Research #Index

Information items carry evidence between BioForge systems without exposing every biological value by tooltip.

- [[Blood Slide]], [[Plasma Sample]], and [[Cell Pellet]] preserve blood-derived evidence.
- [[Medical Report]] stores patient and exact-strain clinical findings.
- [[Gene Imprint]] stores one isolated mutation, symptom, or transmission target after identification.
- [[CRISPR Notes]] stores a reusable sequence or correction template.
- [[Virus Sample]], [[Live Culture Vial]], and [[Dirty Culture Vial]] carry cultivated biological material.
- [[Research Journal|BioForge Research Tablet]] stores player discoveries and renders progression-aware recipes.

See [[Sample Chain]], [[Diagnosis and Medical Records]], and [[NBT Obfuscation and Biological Data]].
