# CRISPR gRNA Cartridge

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Item #Research_Items #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This research item carries or supports biological knowledge that must be earned through the laboratory workflow.
A complete Vaccine Maker program uses fifteen cartridges. Each carries a four-base sequence and a guide role checked against the hidden target.
## Obtaining

- **crafting shaped** (`crispr_cartridge.json`): [[Reinforced Glass]], [[Black Steel Nugget]], `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/redstone`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/paper`, [[Sterile Polymer Sheet]] → 1 × CRISPR gRNA Cartridge.

## Function

- A complete Vaccine Maker program uses fifteen cartridges. Each carries a four-base sequence and a guide role checked against the hidden target.
- **unassigned:** Insert into a Vaccine Maker to assign a fragment
- **tooltip:** Scroll over one of its four bases in the Vaccine Maker; test it on a cultured Petri dish

## Progression connections

- Primarily consumed or interpreted by its linked special interaction rather than ordinary crafting.

## Stored data and safety

- Program, strain, target, quality, and research payloads are server-authoritative and use BioForge biological-data helpers.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:crispr_cartridge`.
- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/crispr_cartridge`.
- Recipes can be replaced without renaming the stable item ID; JEI and the Research Tablet display loaded recipes.
