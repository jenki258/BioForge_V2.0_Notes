> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This research item carries or supports biological knowledge that must be earned through the laboratory workflow.
This is a spent culture container. Sterilize it before safe reuse.
## Obtaining

- Produced by a biological interaction or specialized machine/program. JEI and the Research Tablet show the loaded route.

## Function

- This is a spent culture container. Sterilize it before safe reuse.
- **tooltip:** This vial has been used and is contaminated.
- **tooltip.clean:** Clean it with Ethanol or a Wipe to reuse.

## Progression connections

- Used to produce: [[Live Culture Vial]].

## Stored data and safety

- Program, strain, target, quality, and research payloads are server-authoritative and use BioForge biological-data helpers.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:dirty_culture_vial`.
- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/dirty_culture_vial`.
- Recipes can be replaced without renaming the stable item ID; JEI and the Research Tablet display loaded recipes.
