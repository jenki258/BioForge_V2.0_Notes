# The Beginning of BioForge V2.0

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Wiki #History

BioForge V2.0 rebuilds the original mod as one connected biomedical progression rather than a collection of isolated machines. Diagnosis produces evidence; evidence directs sampling; samples feed cultivation and microscopy; microscopy feeds CRISPR and correction; treatment quality returns to the laboratory through assays; containment and cleanup remain necessary after a patient is treated.

Start with [[Getting Started]] and [[Player Guide]]. For customization, use [[Modpack Maker Guide]] or [[Addon Developer Guide]].
