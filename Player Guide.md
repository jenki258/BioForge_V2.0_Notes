## Early field medicine

Craft the [[Research Journal]] first. It permanently records discoveries and shows visual recipes as related equipment is obtained. Build a basic diagnostic kit: [[Thermometer]], [[Stethoscope]], [[Pulse Oximeter]], [[Otoscope]], [[Mirror]], [[Reflex Hammer]], [[Swab]], [[Clipboard]], and [[Medical Report]]. Together they reveal different parts of a patient instead of collapsing diagnosis into one universal scanner.

Blood work begins with [[Wooden Needle]], then progresses through [[Black Steel Needle]], [[Hardened Needle]], [[Syringe]], [[Test Tube]], and the three typing reagents. Patient knowledge can become stale when the subject's blood data changes, so old results may need to be measured again.

## Building the laboratory

The main progression chain is:

1. Produce [[Black Steel Ingot]] and its components.
2. Assemble engineering parts such as [[Laboratory Frame]], [[Precision Mechanism]], and [[Electronic Control Unit]].
3. Build the [[Centrifuge]], [[Incubator]], and [[Microscope]].
4. Add the [[Barrel Press]], [[Chemical Synthesizer]], [[Sterilization Chamber]], and [[Pharma Mixer]] for the complete chemical chain.
5. Build the [[Vaccine Maker]] after obtaining reliable strain evidence.

## Infection research

Use the Centrifuge to separate blood, the Incubator to cultivate viable samples, and the Microscope to calibrate and reveal biological evidence. A [[Live Culture Vial]] intentionally hides most strain details; proper instruments and information items such as [[Gene Imprint]] and [[CRISPR Notes]] are the intended path to knowledge.

## Treatment and containment

Treatments are specialized:

- [[Strain Vaccine]] attempts a full cure and grants temporary exact-strain immunity.
- [[Mutation Vaccine]], [[Transmission Vaccine]], and [[Symptom Vaccine]] alter one programmed feature.
- [[Random Mutation Vaccine]] deliberately introduces an unknown compatible mutation.
- [[Viral Suppressor Pill]], [[Viral Inhibitor Pill]], and [[Viral Blocker Pill]] grant temporary resistance.
- [[Symptom Suppressant Tablet]] suppresses one penalty without curing its infection.

Use [[Medical Mask]], [[Protective Gloves]], thermal harnesses, and the complete HazCure set to control exposure. Locate invisible hazards with the [[Area Contamination Scanner]] and entrance scanners, then clean rooms with [[Decontamination Flask]] or [[Air Vent]].

## Important expectations

- The incubation period delays symptoms and mutations; an apparently healthy subject may still be infected.
- Walls matter to airborne spread. Doors and open passages connect rooms.
- Contact and environmental infections can remain on blocks.
- Infected food, animal products, drinks, samples, needles, and crafting ingredients can carry a strain onward.
- Better vaccine quality improves treatment reliability and protection duration, but a matching sequence alone does not guarantee a cure.
