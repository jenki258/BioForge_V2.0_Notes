# Magma Bag Harness

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Item #Protective_Equipment #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This component belongs to BioForge's layered containment system; individual equipment covers a specific hazard while full HazCure isolation needs all four suit pieces.
Wear it in the helmet slot to block infection-driven chill and hypothermia damage.
## Obtaining

- **crafting shaped** (`magma_bag.json`): [[Sealed Biofabric]], `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/magma_cream`, [[Thermal Gel]] → 1 × Magma Bag Harness.

## Function

- Wear it in the helmet slot to block infection-driven chill and hypothermia damage.

## Progression connections

- Used to produce: [[HazCure Leggings]].

## Stored data and safety

- It carries no readable biological payload by default, although contaminated ingredients can still propagate a strain.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:magma_bag`.
- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/magma_bag`.
- Recipes can be replaced without renaming the stable item ID; JEI and the Research Tablet display loaded recipes.
