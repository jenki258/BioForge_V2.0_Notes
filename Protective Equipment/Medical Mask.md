# Medical Mask

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Item #Protective_Equipment #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This component belongs to BioForge's layered containment system; individual equipment covers a specific hazard while full HazCure isolation needs all four suit pieces.
It blocks outward airborne shedding, reduces incoming airborne exposure by 80%, and muffles cough and sneeze sounds.
