#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Item #Protective_Equipment #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This component belongs to BioForge's layered containment system; individual equipment covers a specific hazard while full HazCure isolation needs all four suit pieces.
It renders on the arms and blocks outward contact and attack contamination; complete inward protection requires HazCure.
