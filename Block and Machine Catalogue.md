# Block and Machine Catalogue

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Block #Machine #Catalogue #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

## Laboratory machines

- [[Centrifuge]] — eight parallel separation slots.
- [[Incubator]] — cultivation, cloning, and sample generation.
- [[Microscope]] — calibration, scanning, assays, and biological evidence.
- [[Vaccine Maker]] — CRISPR programming, correction, documentation, and treatment synthesis.
- [[Chemical Synthesizer]] — three-input chemical production.
- [[Pharma Mixer]] — five-input pharmaceutical production with a separate waste output.
- [[Sterilization Chamber]] — eight independent in-place sterilization slots.
- [[Barrel Press]] — fruit pressing and Wine Must production.

## Detection and containment

- [[Air Vent]] — passively removes airborne contamination in a ten-block radius.
- [[Viral Scanner Gate]] — complete two-sided walkthrough detector.
- [[Open-Left Viral Scanner]] and [[Open-Right Viral Scanner]] — one-sided gate layouts.
- [[Ceiling Viral Scanner]] — compact overhead detector.

## Environmental biology

- [[Petri Dish]], [[Contaminated Substrate]], [[Colony Core]], [[Infested Block]], [[Microbial Mat]], [[Sporocarp]], and [[Necrotic Patch]].
- [[Black Steel Block]] — compact storage and advanced laboratory construction material.

All placeable machines rotate toward the placing player where their model requires a front. Machine recipes use JSON-backed station definitions and expose their operations to JEI and the Research Tablet.
