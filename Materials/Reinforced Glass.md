# Reinforced Glass

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Item #Materials #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This engineered material is a progression component for workstations, medical hardware, protection, or sterile processing.

## Obtaining

- **crafting shaped** (`reinforced_glass.json`): [[Black Steel Nugget]], `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/glass`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/amethyst_shard` → 4 × Reinforced Glass.

## Function

- Its behavior is defined by its recipe role and linked workstation or equipment system.

## Progression connections

- Used to produce: [[Blood Slide]], [[Chemical Synthesizer]], [[CRISPR gRNA Cartridge]], [[Incubator]], [[Laboratory Frame]], [[Laboratory Glassware]], [[Live Culture Vial]], [[Mirror]], [[Optical Lens]], [[Petri Dish]], [[Sterilization Chamber]], [[Syringe]], [[Thermometer]], [[Test Tube]].

## Stored data and safety

- It carries no readable biological payload by default, although contaminated ingredients can still propagate a strain.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:reinforced_glass`.
- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/reinforced_glass`.
- Recipes can be replaced without renaming the stable item ID; JEI and the Research Tablet display loaded recipes.
