## Overview

This engineered material is a progression component for workstations, medical hardware, protection, or sterile processing.

## Obtaining

- **crafting shaped** (`precision_mechanism.json`): [[Black Steel Plate]], `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/copper_ingot`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/quartz`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/diamond`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/redstone` → 1 × Precision Mechanism.

## Function

- Its behavior is defined by its recipe role and linked workstation or equipment system.

## Progression connections

- Used to produce: [[Biomedical Processor]], [[Bone Saw]], [[Cas PAM Module]], [[Centrifuge]], [[Electronic Control Unit]], [[Laboratory Frame]], [[Respirator Valve]], [[Stethoscope]].

## Stored data and safety

- It carries no readable biological payload by default, although contaminated ingredients can still propagate a strain.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:precision_mechanism`.
- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/precision_mechanism`.
- Recipes can be replaced without renaming the stable item ID; JEI and the Research Tablet display loaded recipes.
