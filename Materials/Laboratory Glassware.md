# Laboratory Glassware

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Item #Materials #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This engineered material is a progression component for workstations, medical hardware, protection, or sterile processing.

## Obtaining

- **crafting shaped** (`laboratory_glassware.json`): [[Reinforced Glass]], [[Black Steel Nugget]], [[Sterile Polymer Sheet]] → 2 × Laboratory Glassware.
- **[[Sterilization Chamber]]** (`laboratory_glassware.json`): `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/glass_bottle` → 1 × Laboratory Glassware in 160 ticks.

## Function

- Its behavior is defined by its recipe role and linked workstation or equipment system.

## Progression connections

- Used to produce: [[Anti-A Reagent]], [[Anti-B Reagent]], [[Anti-D Reagent]], [[Strain Vaccine]], [[Catalyst Vial]], [[Chemical Synthesizer]], [[Decalcification Fluid]], [[Live Culture Vial]], [[Nutrient Medium]], [[Pathogen Reagent]], [[Pharma Mixer]], [[Syringe]], [[Visibility Reagent]].

## Stored data and safety

- It carries no readable biological payload by default, although contaminated ingredients can still propagate a strain.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:laboratory_glassware`.
- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/laboratory_glassware`.
- Recipes can be replaced without renaming the stable item ID; JEI and the Research Tablet display loaded recipes.
