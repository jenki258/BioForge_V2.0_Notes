# Mutation Vaccine

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Item #Medicine #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Documentation status: implemented in V0.54T.

## Overview

This medical item changes infection risk, symptoms, strain parameters, or treatment outcomes.
It adds or removes one programmed mutation through a Gene Imprint and directed action.

- **crafting shapeless** (`mutation_vaccine.json`): [[Strain Vaccine]], `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/amethyst_shard`, `#bioforge:ingredients/minecraft/fermented_spider_eye`, [[Sterile Polymer Sheet]] → 1 × Mutation Vaccine.
