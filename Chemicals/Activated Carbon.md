## Overview

This chemical or processed reagent occupies a defined step in BioForge's sterile production chain.
It connects ordinary fuel processing to filtration, Black Steel, polymers, and decontamination.
## Obtaining

- **smelting** (`activated_carbon.json`): process input → 1 × Activated Carbon.

## Function

- It connects ordinary fuel processing to filtration, Black Steel, polymers, and decontamination.

## Progression connections

- Used to produce: [[Activated Biofilter]], [[Black Steel Blend]], [[Contaminated Substrate]], [[Polymer Resin]], [[Sterile Filter]], [[Pathogen Reagent]].

## Stored data and safety

- It carries no readable biological payload by default, although contaminated ingredients can still propagate a strain.

## Registry and pack integration

- Registry ID: `bioforge:activated_carbon`.
- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/activated_carbon`.
- Recipes can be replaced without renaming the stable item ID; JEI and the Research Tablet display loaded recipes.
