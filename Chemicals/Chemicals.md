# Chemicals

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Chemical #Index #PlayerGuide

BioForge chemistry connects agriculture, black-steel engineering, sterile sample handling, pharmaceuticals, PPE, and vaccine production. Exact recipes are shown in JEI and the Research Tablet.

## Core processing chain

- [[Wine Must]] and [[Ethanol]] begin at the [[Barrel Press]].
- [[Activated Carbon]], [[Sulfuric Acid]], [[Sterilizing Solution]], [[Polymer Resin]], and [[Neutralizing Agent]] are prepared through laboratory processing.
- [[Agar Powder]], [[Nutrient Medium]], and [[Catalyst Vial]] support cultivation.
- [[Surfactant Concentrate]], [[Thermal Gel]], [[Sterile Rubber]], [[Sterile Polymer Sheet]], [[Sterile Filter]], and [[Chemical-Resistant Coating]] feed equipment and PPE.
- [[Decalcification Fluid]] converts hardened bone samples.
- [[Decontamination Flask]] and [[Wipe]] control environmental and equipment contamination.

See [[Chemical Synthesizer]], [[Pharma Mixer]], [[Sterilization Chamber]], and [[Recipe and Tag Compatibility]].
