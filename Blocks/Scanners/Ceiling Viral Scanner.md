# Ceiling Viral Scanner

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Block #Scanner #Redstone #Containment #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

> Registry ID: `bioforge:ceiling_viral_scanner`.

The Ceiling Viral Scanner is a single-block overhead detector for locations where a floor-standing gate is undesirable. It must remain attached to a sturdy underside and scans a narrow column extending about three blocks below itself.

## Output

- Checks every 10 ticks.
- Emits redstone level 15 while a detectable infected living entity occupies the scan column.
- Does not disclose the strain.
- Does not detect a strain with the `scanner_evasion` mutation tag.

It is suitable for alarms, locked doors, quarantine lighting, and automation without side posts.
