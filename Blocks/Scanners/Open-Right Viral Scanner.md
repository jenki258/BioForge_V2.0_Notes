# Open-Right Viral Scanner

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Block #Scanner #Redstone #Containment #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

> Registry ID: `bioforge:open_right_viral_scanner`.

This is the mirrored one-sided scanner: the right wall is open while the opposite frame and overhead beam remain. It uses the same two-block controller, detection volume, ten-tick scan interval, redstone level 15 output, and scanner-evasion rule as the full gate.

Choose left or right by the physical doorway layout; neither variant provides different medical information.
