# Live Culture Vial

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Item #Culture #Incubation #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Registry ID: `bioforge:live_culture_vial`.

**Live Culture Vials** are the practical delivery method for infections.  
They can be **filled** in the Incubator (Culture mode or Blood Duplication mode) and then used to:

- Transfer the infection into a **Syringe** (right‑click a filled syringe with the vial).  
- Infect **food** and **water bottles/potions** (right‑click them with the vial).  
- Inoculate a **Petri Dish** (right‑click a Petri Dish block with the vial).  

After any of these uses, the vial becomes a **Dirty Culture Vial**, which must be cleaned with **Ethanol** or **Wipes** before it can be reused.

## Information policy

The vial stores a complete viable strain through obfuscated server-authoritative data, but its tooltip deliberately reveals only that a sealed genetic profile exists. Use Pathogen/Visibility Reagents, the Incubator, Microscope, Gene Imprints, and records to learn details.

In Creative mode, inoculating a Petri Dish does not replace the held Live Culture Vial with a Dirty Vial. Empty/compatible vial states stack where their NBT allows it.

## Laboratory use

- Incubator input for cloning/expanding cultures.
- Exact-strain evidence for Microscope and Vaccine Maker workflows.
- Can transfer infection into supported blood tools, food, drink, or environmental research targets.
- Spent vials are most reliably restored through the [[Sterilization Chamber]].
