# Nutrient Medium

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Item #Culture #Pharmaceutical #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide #AddonDevelopment

> Registry ID: `bioforge:nutrient_medium`.

**Nutrient Medium** is a consumable item placed in the bottom slots during Catalyst mode.  
It provides the growth substrate that the incubator converts into a Virus Sample.  
Stackable to 64.

## Obtaining

The intended industrial recipe runs in the Pharma Mixer: Laboratory Glassware, Agar Powder, sugar, a compatible potion/water input, and Sterilizing Solution produce three media plus Laboratory Waste in 180 ticks. A crafting fallback also exists in the bundled recipe set.

## Use

Nutrient Medium is a clean biological growth substrate for Incubator catalyst/sample generation and culture progression. It carries no readable strain by default; contamination is stored separately if an infected process writes it.
