# Necrotic Patch

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Block #EnvironmentalInfection #Colony #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

> Registry ID: `bioforge:necrotic_patch`.

The Necrotic Patch is the thin, dead remainder of failed or exhausted colony growth. It is a one-pixel-high, non-occluding black organic layer rather than an active Colony Core.

## Lifecycle

- It may be left when living colony structures die or collapse.
- It does not reproduce or generate colony resources.
- Random ticks give it a 5% chance to disappear, so abandoned biological damage slowly clears itself.
- Because it is fragile, it should be treated as visual evidence of a past event rather than permanent infrastructure.

Modpacks can use it as a visual endpoint for custom environmental behaviors while keeping the stable block ID.
