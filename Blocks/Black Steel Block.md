# Black Steel Block

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Block #Material #BlackSteel #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

> Registry ID: `bioforge:black_steel_block`.

The Black Steel Block is compact storage for nine [[Black Steel Ingot]] items and a structural material in advanced BioForge recipes. It uses a normal block/item registration, requires the correct mining tool, and can be converted back into nine ingots without loss.

## Progression

It represents bulk alloy production rather than a separate biological machine. The Centrifuge recipe consumes one as part of its heavy rotor/base construction, so the first Centrifuge requires more than a few isolated ingots.

## Compatibility

- Preferred ingredient tag: `#bioforge:ingredients/bioforge/black_steel_block`.
- Block recipes are ordinary datapack recipes and may accept another mod's equivalent through tag replacement.
- The block itself stores no infection or machine inventory data.
