# Diagnosis and Medical Records

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Mechanic #Diagnosis #Clipboard #MedicalReport #PlayerGuide

Diagnosis is a multi-tool workflow joined by the Clipboard.

## Workflow

1. Right-click a living entity with the Clipboard to select the patient.
2. Use the Thermometer, Stethoscope, Pulse Oximeter, Otoscope/Mirror, and Reflex Hammer on that subject.
3. Apply blood reagents when blood type is needed.
4. The active Clipboard session records compatible measurements, including temperature.
5. Print a Medical Report with paper or append/copy the readable record into a Book and Quill without opening its GUI.

Reports can stack to 16 when their stored content is stack-compatible. A matching full report can improve Vaccine Maker research and import clinical correction channels; a report belonging to another strain is rejected.

## Copy behavior

Medical Notes/Reports and CRISPR templates have explicit cloning/copy interactions. Source text remains intact when copied into paper, reports, or writable books.

## Data boundary

The player sees measured clinical values, not the entire hidden strain structure. This is why reports support the laboratory rather than replacing microscope research.
