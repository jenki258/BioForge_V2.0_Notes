# Field Workflow

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Mechanic #PlayerGuide #Progression

BioForge's machines form one evidence chain. Skipping a stage is possible in some recipes, but it reduces what the player knows and usually lowers treatment reliability.

1. **Examine:** use field tools and a Clipboard to record the patient's observable state.
2. **Collect:** take blood, a swab, or another compatible sample without contaminating clean equipment.
3. **Separate:** use the Centrifuge when blood components or a slide are required.
4. **Cultivate:** use the Incubator, media, and the appropriate catalyst to preserve or expand the strain.
5. **Identify:** calibrate the Microscope for the inserted item and scan biological evidence.
6. **Program:** prepare 15 CRISPR cartridges and a compatible Cas/PAM module.
7. **Correct:** reconstruct symptoms, transmissions, mutations, and lifecycle evidence in the Vaccine Maker.
8. **Synthesize:** produce the selected vaccine, directed treatment, pill, or suppressant.
9. **Assay:** test uncertain vaccine output against infected blood and scan the combined result.
10. **Contain:** treat the patient, then separately clean items, surfaces, rooms, and entrances.
