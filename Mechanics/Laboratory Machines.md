# Laboratory Machines

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Mechanic #Machine #PlayerGuide

| Station | Inputs and role | Output behavior |
|---|---|---|
| [[Centrifuge]] | Up to eight independent samples | Parallel separation in corresponding slots |
| [[Incubator]] | Primary sample, medium, catalyst | Cultivation, copying, or strain generation across parallel lanes |
| [[Microscope]] | One analyzable item | Persistent per-item calibration and scan/assay evidence |
| [[Vaccine Maker]] | Shared 21-slot inventory across four page families | CRISPR, correction, documentation, synthesis, and redstone start |
| [[Barrel Press]] | Fruit inputs and container | Wine Must production |
| [[Chemical Synthesizer]] | Up to three ingredients | One technical chemical output |
| [[Pharma Mixer]] | Up to five ingredients | Product plus separate laboratory waste |
| [[Sterilization Chamber]] | Parallel contaminated inputs | In-place cleaning or defined sterile conversion |

Machine recipes are data-driven and appear in JEI when installed. The Research Tablet shows structured station recipes as related research unlocks. Processing inventories, calibration state, page state, and biological data are server-authoritative and must survive save/reload.
