# Sample Chain

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Mechanic #Sample #Laboratory #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

BioForge deliberately turns a patient or contaminated environment into several research stages.

## Typical blood route

Needle or Syringe → Test Tube → Centrifuge → Blood Slide / Plasma Sample / Cell Pellet → Microscope.

## Culture route

Infected blood, Virus Sample, environmental swab, Nutrient Medium, Catalyst Vial, and a clean culture container can be processed in the Incubator. A resulting Live Culture Vial contains a sealed biological profile but hides details until analyzed. Using it produces a Dirty Culture Vial that must be sterilized.

## Research outputs

Microscope analysis can populate Gene Imprints, validate visibility/pathogen reagents, reveal symptom-specific evidence, and perform vaccinated-blood assays. The Vaccine Maker consumes evidence, not omniscient tooltips.

## Safety

Blood and strain data are copied only by explicit recipe flags/helpers. Creative-mode use of a Live Culture Vial on a Petri Dish does not replace the held stack with a Dirty Vial.
