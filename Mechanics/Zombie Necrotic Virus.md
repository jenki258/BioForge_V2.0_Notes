# Zombie Necrotic Virus

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Mechanic #NaturalInfection #Zombie #Strain #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

> Strain ID: `bioforge:zombie_necrotic_virus`.

Zombies, husks, drowned, and zombie villagers are default reservoirs for a naturally occurring virus. The rule uses a 100% assignment chance for the listed mobs in the bundled datapack.

## Default profile

- Pathogen: Virus.
- Routes: attack-based and contact-based.
- Infection strength: 0.68.
- Low-temperature symptom enabled.
- Oxygen saturation: 0.82.
- Neural damage: 0.22.
- Microscope visibility: Medium.
- Base mutations: [[Flesh Cravings]] and [[Scanner Evasion]].
- Rare mutations: [[Climbing]] at 3.5% and [[Strong Skin]] at 2.5%.
- Lifecycle: `bioforge:fast_virus`.

The strain can therefore spread through attacks/surfaces, reject non-meat food in infected hosts, evade entrance scanners, and occasionally provide rare zombie-derived genes.

Modpacks may replace the definition or reservoir rule through a higher-priority datapack while preserving saved-world IDs deliberately.
