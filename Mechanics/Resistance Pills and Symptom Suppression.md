## Resistance pills

Viral Suppressor, Inhibitor, and Blocker pills provide three temporary resistance tiers against one programmed strain. They lower the chance that exposure establishes the strain for a limited time. They do not cure an active infection and do not equal the exact immunity effect of a full vaccine.

Quality can change duration and effectiveness, giving the Vaccine Maker a second repeatable product loop beyond vaccines.

## Symptom Suppressant Tablet

A blank tablet becomes programmed in the Vaccine Maker using an identified symptom Gene Imprint. It temporarily disables one symptom penalty but leaves the infection and its diagnostic presence intact.

Duration depends on base server values, tablet/vaccine quality, infection strength, and mutations such as Analgesic Resistance. The item colour follows its programmed symptom so different tablets remain visually distinguishable without unique textures for every case.
