# Strain Naming and Catalogue

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Mechanic #Strain #Naming #Research #Moderation #PlayerGuide

The first researcher to discover an uncatalogued strain is prompted to name it automatically; a command is not required to open the naming screen.

## Identity

The catalogue links a stable strain fingerprint to its chosen public name. Samples and vaccines do not all expose the name: detailed culture evidence and complete medical records may show it, while ordinary Virus Samples, Live Culture tooltips, and vaccines remain intentionally limited.

## Selection

The naming screen accepts manual input and offers a random-name selector. Validation prevents invalid empty names and server moderation commands can rename saved entries when a player chooses abusive text.

## Persistence

Catalogue data is saved server-side and synchronized only where needed. Renaming changes the public label, not the strain fingerprint or treatment match.
