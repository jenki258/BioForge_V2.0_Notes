# Blood System

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Mechanic #Blood #Diagnosis #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

Living entities can carry a persistent blood amount and blood type. Blood loss is gameplay state, not cosmetic sample lore.

## Blood level

- 70–100: normal.
- 40–69: weakness phase.
- 20–39: severe weakness and slowness.
- Below 20: critical weakness, slowness, and blindness.

Regeneration slows as the host becomes more depleted. Needles, syringes, bone extraction, and compatible addon actions can remove blood; transfusion-compatible interactions restore it.

## Collection chain

Needles draw a sample, Test Tubes store it, a Syringe can inject or recover compatible tube blood, and the Centrifuge separates slides/plasma/pellets. Infection data follows only through supported transfer rules and can contaminate reusable equipment.

## Configuration

Entity tags decide which mobs have blood, which use human types, and which diagnostic tools are valid. See [[Blood Mechanic]], [[Blood Types and Compatibility]], and [[Blood Knowledge]].
