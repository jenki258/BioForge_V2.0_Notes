# Samples and Sterility

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Mechanic #Sample #Contamination #PlayerGuide

BioForge containers preserve evidence rather than acting as interchangeable empty bottles. A blood-filled syringe, Test Tube, Blood Slide, Virus Sample, Live Culture Vial, Gene Imprint, and vaccinated-blood assay each represent a different stage of the laboratory chain.

## Handling rules

- Use an empty or biologically compatible destination when transferring blood.
- Preserve exact-strain identity through legitimate separation, cultivation, copying, and assay operations.
- Do not mix unrelated samples merely because their pathogen class or visible name matches.
- Empty test tubes and culture containers may stack where their data is truly identical; filled or individualized samples must not merge destructively.
- Sterilization removes contamination or performs an explicit recipe conversion. It does not invent missing evidence, repair a mislabeled record, or make two strains identical.
- Creative-mode interaction must not replace reusable source samples with dirty containers unless the operation is explicitly designed to do so.
