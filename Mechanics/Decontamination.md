# Decontamination

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Mechanic #Decontamination #Containment #Command #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

Decontamination is explicit because crafting or waiting should not silently clean every hazard.

- Wipes/Ethanol clean supported empty medical tools.
- Sterilization Chamber transforms/cleans compatible stacks in eight independent slots.
- Thrown Decontamination Flask removes surface infection and reduces airborne reservoirs in its configured cube.
- Air Vent passively cleans only AIR_BORNE contamination within 10 blocks.
- The decontamination command removes selected airborne/contact contamination in an administrator-specified radius.
- Datapack block tags choose special decontamination targets.

Cleaning an environment does not cure infected entities, and curing a host does not automatically clean the room.
