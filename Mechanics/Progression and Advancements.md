# Progression and Advancements

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Mechanic #Progression #Advancement #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

V0.54T uses a compact goal-oriented BioForge advancement tree with 21 advancements.

## Progression arc

1. Begin documented research and basic field diagnostics.
2. Complete a patient examination and blood typing.
3. Produce Black Steel and laboratory engineering components.
4. Build Centrifuge, Incubator, and Microscope; obtain cultures and Gene Imprints.
5. Establish Chemical Synthesizer, Barrel Press, Sterilization Chamber, and Pharma Mixer production.
6. Build the Vaccine Maker, document CRISPR, and produce a full-strain vaccine.
7. Detect/contain environmental contamination and perform decontamination.

Advancements signal meaningful milestones rather than listing every item. Research Tablet discoveries provide the finer-grained learning progression.
