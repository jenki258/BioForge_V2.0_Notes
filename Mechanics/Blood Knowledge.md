# Blood Knowledge

#BioForge #BioForge_V2_0 #Build_V0_54T #Mechanic #Blood #Knowledge #Persistence #Command #PlayerGuide

Blood Knowledge is per-player remembered diagnostic data about specific entity subjects.

## Acquisition

Using Anti-A, Anti-B, and Anti-D on a subject or compatible sample records individual reactions. When all three are known, BioForge can present the resolved type in later tooltips and reports.

## Identity and invalidation

Entries are keyed to subject identity rather than display name alone. If the subject's stored blood type changes, the previous knowledge is discarded and must be tested again.

## Administration

The blood-knowledge command can list, copy, clear one, or clear all entries for selected players. These operations are intended for testing, recovery, or pack administration.
