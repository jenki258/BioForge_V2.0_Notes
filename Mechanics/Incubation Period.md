# Incubation Period

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Mechanic #Incubation #Infection #VaccineMaker #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

Incubation is the hidden delay between acquiring an infection and its active symptom/mutation phase. During this time a subject can appear normal even though the infection exists in server data.

## Default behavior

- The profile supplies a base tick duration; the bundled default is 6000 ticks.
- Symptoms, mutation effects, and related penalties wait until incubation completes.
- Spread during incubation is disabled unless the lifecycle profile explicitly enables it.
- Climate can change effective progression according to adaptation and hostile-climate rates.

## Research and treatment

Incubation period is a normal editable Vaccine Maker correction channel rather than an immutable secret constant. A researcher can program it alongside clinical and ecological parameters, document it on correction notes, reset it, or import compatible exact-strain evidence.

## Pack design

Very short incubation creates explosive outbreaks; very long incubation creates investigation and quarantine gameplay. Ensure Research Tablet pages and natural strains communicate the intended pacing without directly exposing every hidden number to players.
