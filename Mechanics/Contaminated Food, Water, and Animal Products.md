# Contaminated Food, Water, and Animal Products

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Mechanic #Food #Water #Animals #Crafting #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

Biological contamination can remain attached to items, so killing or crafting does not silently sterilize a strain.

## Food and crops

Food-borne infection can enter crops, edible drops, and crafted food. Eating a contaminated result performs the relevant exposure check.

## Animals

An animal-route strain can transfer to products such as meat or eggs. Zoonotic mutations and food-borne routes can reinforce this chain.

## Water and drinks

Water-borne infection can enter compatible drinks, water bottles filled from contaminated sources, and brewed potions.

## Craft propagation

Crafting inspects compatible contaminated ingredients and transfers the strongest relevant strain to the result. This supports sandwiches, cooked food, and modded processing chains instead of cleaning them accidentally.

## Compatibility

Pack authors should extend `#bioforge:foods/meat`, crop/entity tags, and ingredient tags for modded content. Sterilization requires an explicit supported recipe or cleaning behavior.
