# Containment and PPE

#BioForge #BioForge_V0_54T #Mechanic #PPE #Containment #PlayerGuide #ModpackGuide

BioForge protection is route-specific. Ordinary armour values do not automatically block infection.

## Equipment

- Medical Mask: blocks all outward airborne shedding, reduces incoming airborne exposure by 80%, and muffles cough/sneeze audio.
- Protective Gloves: block outward contact and attack contamination.
- Ice Bag Harness: blocks infection-driven heat/fever damage.
- Magma Bag Harness: blocks infection-driven chill/hypothermia damage.
- Complete HazCure set: blocks incoming and outgoing airborne/contact/attack exposure, prevents syringe use, blocks thermal symptom damage, and muffles respiratory sounds. All four pieces are required.

HazCure armour defence is intentionally lower than an overpowered end-game combat suit; its reward is biological isolation.

## Tags

Protection behavior uses item tags such as `blocks_outgoing_airborne`, `reduces_incoming_airborne`, contact, syringe, heat/chill, and `hazcure_pieces`. Modpacks can integrate compatible equipment through datapacks.
